Lock-Watcher v1.7.0 adds triggers that fire only while your Mac is locked:

- Display attached while locked
- Location change while locked: the Mac moved 500 m or more since you last unlocked it
- Keyboard or mouse input while locked, after a delay you set from 0 to 10 seconds; a repeated attempt is captured again 30 seconds after a capture
- The new triggers are off by default
- The USB trigger now ignores disk images, network shares, and internal volumes; physical external drives still trigger
- Your saved settings carry over
- Ukrainian localization completed and corrected
